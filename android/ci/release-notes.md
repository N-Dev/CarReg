RoadSight: PlateSight (number-plate reader) and TrafficSight (traffic counter) in one Android app, running on the phone.

**Install:** open this page on the phone and tap **RoadSight.apk**. When Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (once), then tap Install. Later versions install over this one and keep your history and settings. If Play Protect says the app is unknown, tap **More details → Install anyway**.

What's in it: live plate reading with a **watchlist** (a notification when a plate you're watching for is seen) and an ignore list for your own cars; photos and **videos**; the traffic counter (lines across the road or for a road running away from you, **counting with the screen off**, counting a **video**, zoom, portrait or landscape) with charts, CSV and the council report, also **across several days**; **backup and restore**; **debug mode**; and a speed test that finds the fastest way to run the AI on your phone. Settings → About → Check for updates tells you when there's a newer version.

Needs Android 8 or later on a 64-bit phone.
