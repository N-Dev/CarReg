RoadSight: PlateSight (number-plate reader) and TrafficSight (traffic counter) in one Android app, running on the phone.

**Install:** open this page on the phone and tap **RoadSight.apk**. When Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (once), then tap Install. Later versions install over this one and keep your history and settings.

Needs Android 8 or later on a 64-bit phone.
