TrafficSight

Count the traffic on your road

Stand the phone at a window, side-on to the road and plugged in. It counts cars, vans, buses, bikes, people and dogs, and times vehicles between two lines for their speed. Nothing is recorded.

Set up the counting lines

Put the phone side-on to the road. Drag line A and line B across the road at two marks you can measure between, like lamp posts, gateposts or road markings, 10–30 m apart.

Settings

Detection modelStandard is more accurate; Light is quicker on slower phones. Auto picks one by timing your phone.
Frame rateFastest analyses every frame the phone can keep up with. Cooler analyses about 10 a second, which is still plenty for counting and speeds, and keeps the phone cooler on long or warm days.
SensitivityHigher picks up smaller and further things, with more false alarms.
Dim the screen while countingSaves battery and heat. Tap to wake.
PrivacyThe picture is analysed on this phone and thrown away. TrafficSight keeps only what passed and when: no images, video or number plates, and nothing leaves the phone unless you share a report.
